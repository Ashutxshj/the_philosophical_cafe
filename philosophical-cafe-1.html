"My journey into philosophy began with a search for answers, but I discovered something far more powerful: the art of asking the right questions."

I am Chetna, a certified Philosophical Counselor dedicated to helping you challenge conditioned beliefs and declutter your mind. I believe our lives are shaped by our thoughts, decisions, and attitudes, and that through guided reflection, we can understand these core components, leading to greater self-awareness and alignment.

My approach is not about providing quick fixes or promising constant happiness. I empower you to comprehend your problems deeply, encouraging a sense of peace and understanding from which authentic clarity can emerge.

Sessions are one-on-one, fully online via Google Meet, and available to anyone across India. No prior knowledge of philosophy required. Only a willingness to think honestly.
